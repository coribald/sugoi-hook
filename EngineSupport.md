## Overview

Sugoi Hook is now a Luna-only build.

This document is a reference list for:

- engines covered by Sugoi Hook custom or community hook support
- engines covered by Luna Hook itself

It is intended as a practical compatibility reference, not a promise that every individual game on every engine will hook perfectly without manual selection or tuning.

## Notes

- Luna coverage is broad, but hook quality still varies game by game.
- Manual hook selection may still be needed even when an engine family is known to be supported.
- Hook Concatenation remains important for games that split speaker and dialogue across separate hooks.
